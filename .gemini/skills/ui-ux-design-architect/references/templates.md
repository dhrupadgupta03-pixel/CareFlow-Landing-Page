# Design Document Templates

## 1. UI/UX Design Strategy Document
Structure:
- **Header:** Project Name, Date, Status.
- **Context & Problem:** Core friction and user pain points.
- **User Personas:** Detailed breakdown of roles and their specific goals/panic points.
- **Design Goals & Principles:** Experience goals and the "Laws" of the system.
- **Information Architecture:** Sitemap and Mental Models.
- **Design System Foundation:** Semantic colors, Typography, Spacing/Grid.
- **Interaction Model:** Happy path and State management (Empty/Loading/Error).
- **A11y & Technical Constraints:** Accessibility standards and framework alignment.

## 2. Competitive Landscape & Design Benchmarking
Structure:
- **Executive Summary:** Overview of the market design standard.
- **Competitor Analysis (4 Competitors):**
    - **Visual Identity:** Color palettes, typography, and "vibe".
    - **Key UI Elements:** Unique components or navigation patterns.
    - **Design Principles:** Observed UX priorities (e.g., "Minimalist" vs "Data Dense").
    - **Pros/Cons:** What to emulate and what to avoid.
- **Synthesis:** Key takeaways for our project.
