## 2. Key Features

### 🩺 For Doctors: The AI Prescription Digitizer

- Never type a prescription again.
- Take a photo of your handwritten notes, and our advanced AI (Gemini 1.5 Flash) instantly segments, reads, and digitizes them securely using a comprehensive medicine database.
- Save time, eliminate repetitive data entry, and reduce prescription errors.

### 🏥 For Receptionists: Streamlined Workflow

- Manage the entire clinic queue seamlessly.
- Track patient flow from walk-in to final checkout on a dedicated queue management dashboard.

### 👤 For Patients: Healthcare at Your Fingertips

- Access your digitized prescriptions securely, anytime and anywhere.
- View your history and interact with the clinic effortlessly.

## 3. How It Works (The Digitizer Engine)

1. **Write:** The Doctor handwrites a prescription naturally.
2. **Scan:** Capture the prescription image via the CareFlow app.
3. **Digitize:** Our backend engine processes the image using state-of-the-art OCR and cross-references an extensive drug database (RAG).
4. **Update:** The patient’s digital record is updated instantly with high accuracy.
